Product Overview

The MP Doors MasterPiece® Series Sliding Patio Door is engineered to be the last patio door you'll ever need. Unlike traditional wood doors that can rot, warp, and swell when exposed to moisture, the MasterPiece door is constructed from high-performance composite materials that are 100% waterproof.

This 72 in. x 80 in. unit features a smooth white finish that requires no painting (though it is paintable if desired). The system comes pre-hung for easier installation and includes energy-efficient Low-E glass to help maintain indoor temperatures year-round.

Lifetime Reliability
