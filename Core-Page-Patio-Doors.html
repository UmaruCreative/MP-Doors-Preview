Patio Door FAQs

Will MP Doors patio doors rot or swell over time?
No. Composite construction does not absorb moisture, helping the door maintain alignment and sealing through seasonal changes.
Are MP Doors patio doors energy efficient?
Yes. Many configurations include double-pane Low-E glass with Argon gas and ENERGY STAR® certification where applicable.
Are these doors designed for large patio openings?
Yes. MP Doors patio systems are engineered to support wide spans and heavy glass panels without sacrificing smooth operation.
Are MP Doors patio doors factory assembled?
Yes. Doors are assembled in controlled factory environments and delivered as pre-hung systems to reduce installation risk.
Where can I purchase MP Doors patio doors?
MP Doors patio doors are available nationwide through The Home Depot, including measurement and installation services.
