MP Doors Entry Doors

Entry Door Systems Designed to Resist Water, Wear, and Everyday Abuse

An entry door is opened more than any other door in the home. MP Doors entry door systems are engineered to withstand daily use, moisture exposure, and seasonal movement—without swelling, sticking, or constant maintenance.

Does Your Entry Door Stick, Draft, or Need Constant Repainting?

Many entry door problems appear gradually. Frames absorb moisture, paint begins to peel, and small alignment issues turn into daily frustration. These failures often come from materials that were never designed for long-term exposure to weather.

Entry Door FAQs

Will MP Doors entry doors swell or rot over time?
No. Composite frames and fiberglass panels do not absorb moisture, helping maintain alignment and appearance through seasonal changes.
Are MP Doors entry doors suitable for harsh climates?
Yes. Materials are selected specifically to resist moisture, temperature swings, and long-term exposure.
Are these doors energy efficient?
Many configurations include insulated cores and ENERGY STAR® certified options to help reduce heat transfer.
Do MP Doors entry doors require frequent maintenance?
No. Unlike traditional wood doors, composite and fiberglass construction significantly reduces repainting and repair needs.
Where can I purchase MP Doors entry doors?
MP Doors entry doors are available nationwide through The Home Depot, including professional measurement and installation services.
